DEFENCE ACCREDITATION

Fastener Defence Standards: 02-862

COFAST supplies high-integrity fasteners conforming directly to Ministry of Defence and naval dockyard requirements:

  • Defence Standard 02-862 Part 1: Fasteners for naval marine surface and submarine installations
  • Defence Standard 02-862 Part 2 Issue 3: Metric and unified high tensile naval fasteners
  • Defence Standard 02-862 Part 3 Issue 3: Non-ferrous and corrosion-resistant fasteners
  • British Standards: All relevant BS specifications for bolts, nuts, studs, and washers
  • AISI / SAE: Conformance to American Iron and Steel Institute / Society of Automotive Engineers standards
