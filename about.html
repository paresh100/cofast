About Company Fasteners Ltd

For over a quarter of a century, COFAST has supplied precision threaded fastenings, industrial bearings, and engineering adhesives to the UK's most critical industrial facilities.

A COMMITMENT TO TOTAL SERVICE

"A service that meets the exacting criteria of our clients through total quality management"

Company Fasteners (COFAST) is a leading independent distributor of fasteners to manufacturing, marine, aerospace, and construction clients across the United Kingdom and internationally.

The company has been established for over 25 years, making it one of the most respected and dependable industrial suppliers in the region. Operating from our purpose-built 600m² logistics centre in Plympton, located minutes from the main A38 trunk road, we guarantee effortless connectivity to the national motorway and pallet networks.

Close liaison between our sales engineers and our clients' technical teams remains central to COFAST's operational ethos. Our rapid response service enables major OEMs and maintenance teams to function leanly with reduced buffer stocks without compromising production capacity.
