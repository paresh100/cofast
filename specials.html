MANUFACTURE TO DRAWING

Tight Tolerances. Non-Standard Pitches. Safety-Critical Integrity.

Many safety-critical applications in defence, nuclear power, subsea oil & gas, and aerospace demand fastener dimensions and thread profiles that simply do not exist in standard DIN or ISO tables.

COFAST manages the complete manufacturing cycle—from certified raw bar procurement and precision CNC lathe turning through to heat treatment, thread rolling, passivating, and dimensional CMM inspection.

Tolerances to ±0.01mm

High-precision turning for aerospace jigs, instrumentation, and turbine fixtures.

Bar Diameters 3mm–120mm

Capable of producing miniature electronics fasteners up to heavy marine drive studs.

Typical CNC Bar-Turned Components

  • Engineers Studs & Tie Rods: Double-ended studs, wafer head studs, waist studs
  • Non-Standard Socket Screws: Extended lengths, undercut heads, shoulder screws
  • Special Nuts & Spacers: Castle nuts, slotted round nuts, bespoke standoffs
  • Precision Dowel Pins: Hardened and ground pull-out dowel pins (DIN 7979)
  • Obsolete Thread Forms: BSF, BA, British Standard Whitworth, ACME threads

MATERIALS EXPERTISE

Exotic Superalloys for Extreme Operating Environments

Certified raw materials with full mill provenance and EN 10204 3.1 certification.

NICKEL-CHROMIUM

Inconel 625 & 718

Exceptional creep resistance and oxidation tolerance up to 980°C. Widely specified for gas turbines, aerospace rocket engines, and nuclear reactors.

NICKEL-COPPER

Monel 400 & K-500

Virtually immune to saltwater chloride stress corrosion cracking. The alloy of choice for marine propeller shafts, subsea valves, and offshore splash zones.

HIGH CORROSION

Super Duplex 2507

Yield strength exceeding 550 MPa with superior pitting resistance (PREN > 42) in aggressive petrochemical and desalinisation environments.

LIGHTWEIGHT STRENGTH

Titanium Gr2 & Gr5

High strength-to-weight ratio with total resistance to seawater and chemical attack. Widely applied in marine racing, aerospace, and medical implants.
